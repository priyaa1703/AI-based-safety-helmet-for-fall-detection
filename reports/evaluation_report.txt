Why false negatives matter more than false positives for FALL:
  - A false negative means a miner is hurt (maybe unconscious, trapped) and
    nobody is told. The cost is delayed rescue, which can mean death.
  - A false positive means a spurious alarm. The cost is a supervisor
    checking on a miner who is fine - annoying, but harmless.
  - So for deployment, bias the decision toward catching falls, e.g. raise an
    alert when P(FALL) > ~0.3 rather than waiting until FALL is the arg-max,
    and accept more false alarms.
